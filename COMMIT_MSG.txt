Move Original File to top-right, rework PiP to spec, add hover-aware auto-hide

Three changes to the player chrome.

"Original File" moves from the middle of the picture to the top-right
window-controls cluster. The bottom-centre pill is gone entirely: it held only
the "Requested by" credit, which is not needed here. buildPill, pill_,
lblRequester_ and requester_ are deleted, and setMedia loses its requester
parameter (every caller was already passing QString()).

PiP follows the spec: mute top-left, restore + close top-right, a large
centred play button (84 px, 34 px white glyph), time labels and a thin scrub
at the bottom. The volume slider is removed from the top row. The auto-hide
now stands down while the pointer is on the float: Enter sets hovered_ and
cancels the timer, Leave starts it again, and hideControls() returns early
while hovered_. Without this the controls faded out from under a pointer that
was reaching for one.

The interaction probe is extended to 21 checks and all pass: the corner
placement (top-right, visible), every PiP element, both hover states, and the
full Esc ladder.
